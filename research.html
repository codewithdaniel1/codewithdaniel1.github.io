Research experience

OSIRIS Research Lab · New York University

My work at OSIRIS includes honeypot research, wireless security analysis, and AI security education. I explore what honeypots reveal about how exposed services are targeted and how capture the flag (CTF) challenges can help learners practice investigating AI security risks.

Research focus

I’m interested in what defenders can learn from activity observed in real systems and how to turn those observations into practical security exercises.

01

Honeypot research

Exploring honeypots as a way to observe suspicious activity and understand how network-facing services are targeted.

02

Wireless security analysis

Using wardriving and wireless analysis to examine network exposure and security conditions in real environments.

03

Evolving CTFs for AI

Exploring how capture-the-flag challenges can evolve to teach and assess emerging AI security topics.

04

SLM-driven redesign

Investigating how small language models can support the redesign of challenge scenarios and learning experiences.
