Research focus

My research looks at how data can make security activity easier to understand and investigate. I’m especially interested in connecting authentication events into useful behavioral context and making analytical results clear enough for people to assess the evidence behind them.

01

Honeypot research

Exploring honeypots as a way to observe suspicious activity and understand how network-facing services are targeted.

02

Wireless security analysis

Using wardriving and wireless analysis to examine network exposure and security conditions in real environments.

03

Evolving CTFs for AI

Exploring how capture-the-flag challenges can evolve to teach and assess emerging AI security topics.

04

SLM-driven redesign

Investigating how small language models can support the redesign of challenge scenarios and learning experiences.
