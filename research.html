Research focus

My research looks at how data can make security activity easier to understand and investigate. I’m especially interested in connecting authentication events into useful behavioral context, then testing whether the resulting signals help distinguish suspicious activity from normal operations.

I also care about explainability: an analytical result should give an investigator enough context to understand why it was surfaced, what evidence supports it, and where uncertainty remains.

OSIRIS Research Lab

I’m part of the OSIRIS Research Lab at NYU. The lab brings together people working on offensive security, security education, and research.
