About me

I like turning a vague question into something I can inspect. That might mean tracing an unusual authentication pattern through a graph, repairing a file one byte at a time in a CTF, or building a tool that removes repetitive work.

At NYU, I’m part of the OSIRIS Research Lab. Outside the lab, I contribute to open-source tools and work on projects of my own.
