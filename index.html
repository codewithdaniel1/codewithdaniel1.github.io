01 / ABOUT

A little about me

I’m drawn to the parts of a problem that are easy to overlook: a pattern in a dataset, a strange path through a network, or a tool that could be simpler to use. My work sits between security research, software, and data.

At NYU, I’m part of the OSIRIS Research Lab community and compete with NYUSEC. Outside the lab, I make open-source tools and contribute to projects I use and admire.
