N0v4CTF 2026.

NovaRun’s worldwide online capture-the-flag competition, with challenge writeups in digital forensics and cryptography.

November 27–28, 202624-hour online Jeopardy CTF

The competition

N0v4CTF was free to enter and opened at 17:00 UTC on November 27. It closed 24 hours later. Teams could have any number of members and choose from six tracks covering web exploitation, binary exploitation, reverse engineering, cryptography, digital forensics, and miscellaneous challenges including OSINT.

Most flags used the NVR{...} format. Challenge point values fell as more teams solved them. TryHackMe sponsored Premium vouchers for the top three teams and the best writeup.
