COMPETITION / 2026

ISACA Student Chapters CTF.

My team, NYUSEC, finished first in a student capture the flag competition organized by the ISACA student chapters at Fordham University and Yeshiva University’s Katz School of Science and Health.

1st place · NYUSEC3,770 team pointsNovember 6, 2026

The result

The final scoreboard listed NYUSEC in first place with 3,770 points. Its team-member breakdown credited me with 1,400 points. My recorded solves covered cryptography and forensics challenges.

The competition

The Fordham and Yeshiva ISACA student chapters held the event at Fordham University’s Lincoln Center campus in New York City on November 6, 2026. It brought together NYC university students for hands-on offensive and defensive cybersecurity challenges in mixed-experience teams.
