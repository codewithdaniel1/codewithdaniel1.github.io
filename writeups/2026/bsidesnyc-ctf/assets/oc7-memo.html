OC-7 Internal Memorandum

Accessible HTML transcription of the memo referenced by the BSidesNYC CTF challenge “Classified.”

Restricted · OC-7

To: Office of Continuity, OC-7

From: Production Security Review

Subject: CIPHER production records and the Poole incident

Distribution: Internal review copy

Purpose

This memorandum consolidates production records for the CIPHER film series and documents the unauthorized disclosure associated with the Lisbon production. The records indicate that the matter concerned restricted production information rather than an on-screen role.

Series records

The CIPHER productions used compartmentalized crews and production files. Records for The Lisbon Frequency include second-unit dialogue work attributed to Derek Poole. The available filmography contains no credited work for Poole before or after this production. His limited engagement explains the discrepancy between the production notes and public credits.

Poole incident

Poole was removed from the OC-7 production following an unauthorized disclosure. References on the CipherWiki talk page to a “retired” Agent Poole correspond to this production incident. Earlier discussion of his credit was moved to the Canon Disputes board.

Annex A · Production history

Production records associate the Lisbon unit with restricted location and dialogue material. Second-unit dialogue was handled separately from the principal cast record. This distinction is relevant when comparing the internal file with public film credits.

Annex B · Handling notes

Access to OC-7 materials was limited. Copies of production documentation were not to be circulated outside the designated review group. The incident report records a breach of that handling restriction.

Annex C · Disposition

D. Poole · Terminated from OC-7 production, February 1967.
Reason: Unauthorized disclosure.
Current status: See file LAMPREY.
